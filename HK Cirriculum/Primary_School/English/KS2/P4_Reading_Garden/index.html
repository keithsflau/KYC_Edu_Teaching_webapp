花園通訊KS2 · 小四 P4 · Reading

學習目標 Learning objective

讀一篇短通訊，找出時間、原因和詞義。答錯時會指出文章哪一句才是根據。

Read a short newsletter, then find the time, the reason, and what a word means in the text.

Last Friday, our class visited the school garden. We grew lettuce and cauliflower.

My favourite vegetable is cauliflower because it has a white head. Lettuce has green leaves.

After the visit, Ms Lee gave us a quiz. The quiz was necessary because some of us mixed up lettuce and cauliflower.

We also talked about nutrition. Nutrition means the goodness food gives our bodies.

Next week, we will write a shopping list for soup.
