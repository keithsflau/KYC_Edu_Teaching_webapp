用 because 寫兩句KS2 · 小五 P5 · Writing

學習目標 Learning objective

寫兩句：第一句說 favourite snack，第二句用 because 給原因。使用英式拼寫、大寫和句號。

Write two sentences about your favourite snack. Use because, British spelling, a capital letter, and a full stop.

My favourite snack is a biscuit. I like it because it is crunchy.
