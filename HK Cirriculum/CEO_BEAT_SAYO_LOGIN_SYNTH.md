## 1. Honest Merged Verdict: The 5-Line Audit
We successfully executed staggered, anti-lockout audits across all five Sayo subjects. The reality of their platform requires an immediate pivot in our competitive strategy.

### 🔴 The Threats: Chemistry, Physics, Math, English
- **Chemistry (`chem.sayo.ai`)**: It is a real, stateful simulator. Their Acid-Base Titration lab features interactive apparatus controls, dynamic indicators, and live titration curve charting. It is a highly competent product.
- **Physics (`phy.sayo.ai`)**: Also a real simulation utilizing `<canvas>` and `<svg>`. It features real-time sliders and interactive UI for kinetic theory and optics.
- **Math (`maths.sayo.ai`)**: An OCR-driven fortress. It accurately reads handwritten DSE math and provides step-by-step method marks. It is a highly polished rote exam churn engine.
- **English (`english.sayo.ai`)**: A deep moat of reading/listening libraries, AI-graded writing, and highly interactive speaking mock exams (both AI and live 4-person).

### 🟢 The Opportunities: Biology & Conceptual Gaps
- **Biology (`bio.sayo.ai`)**: **Vaporware.** The platform is a hollow frontend shell. "Experiments" redirect to Chemistry, and animations are blank canvases with dead buttons. Sayo has zero functional Bio labs.
- **Conceptual Scaffolding**: While Sayo's Chem and Phys labs are interactive, they lack our **Teach-From-Zero (TFZ)** pedagogical scaffolding. They drop students into a lab or a drill; we guide them through the underlying concepts (A1-A8) *before* the simulation.

## 2. Strategic Pivot: Our Wedge
1. **Double Down on TFZ in Science:** Since Sayo's Science simulations are visually competitive, our differentiator must be *pedagogy*. We must beat their UX in teaching clarity. Our interactive manipulatives must be framed within structured, step-by-step mastery pathways.
2. **Exploit the Bio Gap:** We have a massive head start in Biology. We must immediately launch interactive Bio labs to dominate the space Sayo is faking.
3. **Conceptual Math, Not Drill Math:** Do not fight Sayo's OCR volume war. Focus entirely on pre-drill visual understanding (e.g., interactive 3D trigonometry, locus dragging) to intercept struggling students.
4. **Avoid the English Fortress:** Competing on English assessment and mocks is a trap. Conserve resources and maintain focus on STEM.
