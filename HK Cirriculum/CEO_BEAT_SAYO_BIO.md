# Sayo Biology Platform - Honest Depth Analysis

**Executive Summary:** Sayo Biology is currently **completely fake/non-functional vaporware**. It is a hollow frontend shell and represents zero threat to our interactive Bio labs at this time.

## Honest Depth vs. Our Bio Labs

- **Mocked Interface Only:** The `bio.sayo.ai` subdomain displays an impressive-looking curriculum list (Enzyme Activity, Cell Cycle, Osmosis, etc.), but there are no actual biology simulations backing them.
- **Cross-Domain Redirection:** Clicking on the interactive "Experiment" tab for the *Catalase Enzyme Activity* lab abandons the Biology site and completely redirects the user to the Chemistry platform's *Acid-Base Titration* lab.
- **Empty Shells:** Other interactive modules (e.g., the "Animation" tab for *Mitosis & Cell Division*) contain static placeholder buttons ("Play Animation", "Reset", "prophase", etc.) but have absolutely no `<canvas>`, visual SVGs, or interactive elements present. The simulation area is literally blank.
- **Competitive Advantage:** We have a massive head start. Sayo has clearly prioritized their Chemistry platform (which has functional labs like the Titration simulator), but their Biology product is currently just hardcoded UI mocks and placeholders.
