## 3. Subject Wing head-to-head (Deep Audit)

*Audit Note: Sayo's subject platforms (`sayo.ai/subjects` redirecting to `aail.ai/platforms`) are heavily gated behind SSO (Google/Microsoft) and school B2B logins. Analysis is based on their public marketing, landing pages (e.g., `maths.sayo.ai`), and app store presence.*

### Science: Our Interactive Labs vs Sayo DSE Experiments
- **Sayo's Approach**: Listed as "Science Experiments" (Physics, Chemistry, Biology). Based on their core tech, these likely rely on chat-based "build canvas apps", text-based flashcards, and LLM-generated quizzes. They lack deep, bespoke interactive manipulatives.
- **Our Advantage**: **Teach-From-Zero (TFZ)**. Our Form 4-6 Science labs (e.g., `Chemistry/Form 5/09_Chemical_Equilibrium_Kc`) offer A1-A8 guided pedagogical scaffolding and deep interactive manipulatives. We actually simulate the science; they likely just quiz it.

### Math: Our Math Labs vs Sayo Maths
- **Sayo's Approach**: `maths.sayo.ai` is highly polished for drilling. Features include OCR for handwritten work, step-by-step AI grading aligned to DSE marking schemes (method marks), and an AI sidekick ("Calculo") for hints. Infinite drills and mock exams.
- **Our Advantage**: Sayo wins on sheer volume of DSE-aligned drills and OCR grading. We need to counter with conceptual Math manipulatives (visualizing geometry/calculus) rather than competing purely on drill volume.

### English & Chinese: The Gap
- **Sayo's Approach**: Extremely comprehensive. 
  - **English**: *Penso* (AI writing correction), *Sayo Reading & Listening*, *Sayo Speaking Live* (4-person DSE Paper 4 mock), and *Sayo Speaking App* (AI voice tutor with instant pronunciation/grammar feedback).
  - **Chinese**: *Makwan* (墨韻 - AI Chinese writing training).
- **Our Status**: We are significantly thinner here. Sayo has built a moat in language assessment and oral practice.
