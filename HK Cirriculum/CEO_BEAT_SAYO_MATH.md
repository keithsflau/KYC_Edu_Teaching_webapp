## 1. Sayo Maths: The Threat
- **Core Loop**: Infinite drills, auto-generated mocks, "Calculo" AI sidekick.
- **Killer Tech**: High-precision OCR natively reading handwritten/printed DSE maths.
- **Assessment**: Step-by-step AI grading. Awards DSE method marks/partial credit; rejects mere final-answer checks.
- **Coverage**: Exhaustive DSE syllabus (Arithmetics, Algebra, Geometry, Coord Geo, Stats).

## 2. Sayo's Blind Spots
- **Testing > Teaching**: Rote exam churn engine. Weak on fundamental pedagogical scaffolding.
- **Static UI**: Relies on text-based chat and flat 2D renders. Zero interactive manipulatives.
- **Late-Stage Dependency**: Only serves students ready to drill. Abandons students needing "from-scratch" visual instruction.

## 3. Our Counter: Conceptual Math Labs
- **Pre-Empt the Drill**: Win the foundational learning phase. Intercept students *before* they rely on Sayo's drill farm.
- **Visual Dominance**: Deploy dynamic, interactive manipulatives (e.g., live loci dragging, manipulable 3D trigonometry). Exploit Sayo's flat canvas weakness.
- **Teach-From-Zero (TFZ)**: Mandate A1-A8 guided pedagogy. Replace generic "Ask Calculo" hints with structured, interactive mastery pathways.
- **Diagnostic OCR**: Do not fight an OCR volume war. Repurpose OCR strictly as a diagnostic funnel to route struggling students directly into relevant conceptual labs.
